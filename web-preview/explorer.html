FIVE STAGES

01 / GET STARTED

A tool layer.
Not another model.

OmniForge organizes skills and permissioned workflows for agents such as Codex, Claude Code, Cursor and others. It does not bundle a model, auto-install 112 external projects, or replace an autonomous coding agent.
